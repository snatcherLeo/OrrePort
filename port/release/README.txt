OrrePort Beta 0.1 (first playtest)
==================================

Pokémon Colosseum, running natively on PC, rebuilt from its own code. Bring your own disc.
You need your own copy of the game. Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit).

Getting started
1. Start OrrePort.exe.
   Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" (OrrePort isn't signed by a publisher):
   click More info, then Run anyway.
2. Choose Add a disc and pick your own Pokémon Colosseum disc image (US, GC6E01): ISO, GCM, CISO or RVZ.
   You can also drop it onto the window, or put it in the "Pokemon Colosseum\ROM" folder.
   OrrePort checks it the first time (a few seconds).
3. Press PLAY.

Other languages
German, French, Italian and Spanish play from your own European disc, Japanese from your own Japanese disc.
Add it on the hub's Language discs panel, or put it in "Pokemon Colosseum\ROM Other Regions".

Everything stays in this folder
Your saves, settings and reports live inside this folder; nothing is installed in Windows.
To move OrrePort, or back it up, copy the whole folder.
This is a playtest: keep a copy of any save you care about.

Something wrong?
Press F8 in the game, or use Report a problem on the hub: OrrePort makes a report you can share on the OrrePort
Discord: [Discord invite link, added on release day]

Also here
OrrePort Studio.exe opens OrrePort Studio, the editor beside the game (an early preview).
Updates are offered inside the app; nothing is downloaded until you choose to.
