# pcdata.py: main-program tables the PC build does not carry. Each is defined without contents (type, size, alignment
# only) and filled at start-up from the player's own main.dol (pc_disc_dol_read, pc_disc_check.c), checked by CRC-32,
# before the pointer slots are filled. the reference playthrough Part 1 item 2c step 5 (docs/port/disc-data-loading.md), 2026-11-01.
# Format: NAME [SIZE]   (SIZE overrides the symbol's size when the table runs on past the next symbol)
